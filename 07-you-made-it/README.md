# 07 — You made it. Now what.

You made it through the path. While people on LinkedIn are still
"leveraging AI for synergistic outcomes," you actually know how the
thing works.

Here's what you can do that they can't:

## what you can do now

- **Install Claude Code** on any computer that lets you install
  things.
- **Make Claude do real work for you**: write files, organize
  folders, draft the thing you've been procrastinating on.
- **Teach Claude about a project** with CLAUDE.md, so you stop
  re-explaining yourself every session.
- **Install other people's skills** with a couple of commands.
  Borrow what smarter people figured out.
- **Plug Claude into outside apps** with MCP, when the built-in
  tools aren't enough.

## when someone says "AI"

You can now hold your own. Some honest translations:

- "AI-powered" usually means Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok (or
  whatever the newest model is when you're reading this) wrapped
  around something.
- "Will AI take my job?" depends on whether you understand it.
  You're now in the camp that does.

You don't have to be the loudest. You just have to be the one who
knows the difference between a prompt, a skill, an MCP, and a
CLAUDE.md.

## where to go from here

Those podcast recommendations Claude pulled for you back in chapter
06? Go listen to them. You already did the hard part of finding
them.

That's the book. Go play.
